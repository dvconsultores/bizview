# View a report

## What it is for

A report shows a dashboard's indicators with their comparison, trend, rankings and detail
tables.

## Who can use it

Anyone with access to that dashboard.

## How to

1. Open a dashboard from [Open Dashboards](app:/tableros).
2. At the top are the **Indicators** with their value and the comparison with the previous
   period. An indicator with no previous value is marked as new.
3. Below come the trend, the rankings and the tables. **View data** shows the numbers of a
   chart.
4. Under the title one line summarises the **Period**, the date range it is compared with and when
   the data was updated. Applied filters show as chips you remove with one tap.
5. When the report declares its own composition, the cards appear in the order and with the width
   its author chose; on a phone they stack in that same order. Without it, the dashboard composes
   itself.
6. Every chart carries a sentence summing up what it shows. It is read below the chart and screen
   readers announce it as well.

## Options

- Each card's menu lets you download its data.
- **AI summary**: when the installation has AI, **Generate summary** writes a summary from
  aggregate figures; it never sends records.
- A direct report reads "Live data · … · viewed …": data comes from the source at that moment.
  If the source does not answer, the last saved data is shown with its time.
- If a card cannot be shown, a short notice explains it in its place; there is never a blank gap.

## Common problems

- "No data in the period.": change the period in **Parameters**.
