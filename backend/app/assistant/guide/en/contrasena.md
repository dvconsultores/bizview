# Change your password

## What it is for

Change your password whenever you want, or when BizView asks you to because the one you have is
temporary.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account.

## How to

1. Open [Open Change password](app:/cambiar-clave), or from your **Profile** choose
   **Change password**.
2. Type the **Current password** (if it is temporary, the **Temporary password** you were given).
3. Type the **New password** and repeat it in **Confirm new password**.
4. Press **Change password**.

## Options

The new password must meet the **Password requirements**:

- **Between 10 and 128 characters**
- **Does not contain your email**
- **Different from current**

If your password is temporary, the screen is called **Create your password** and you cannot use
the app until you change it.

## Common problems

- Changing it signs out your other sessions on other devices; sign in there again with the new
  password.
- "Passwords do not match.": type the same password in both fields.
