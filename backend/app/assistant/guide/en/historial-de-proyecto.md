# Project history

## What it is for

It keeps every change of every project file: who made it (a person, the agent with its command,
an import or an external change) and lets you go back.

## Who can use it

Only the **Superuser**.

## How to

1. In the project editor press **History**.
2. Filter with **Filter by file** or **Filter by spec**.
3. Each change shows the author, the command (for example `/speckit-plan`) and the conversation.
4. To see differences, pick two versions with **From** and **To** and press **Compare**.
5. To go back to a version, press **Restore**: a new change is created, nothing is deleted.

## Options

- A spec's **Timeline** shows its way through the stages and the commands that changed it.
- **External change**: the file changed on disk outside BizView.
- The project's applies also appear in the history.
