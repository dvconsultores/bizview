# Confirm your password

## What it is for

Before a sensitive action BizView asks for your password again, so nobody can do it from a
session you left open.

## Who can use it

Administrators and the **Superuser**, who are the ones doing sensitive actions.

## How to

1. When **Confirm your password** appears, type your password.
2. Press **Confirm**. The action then continues by itself.

The confirmation lasts 5 minutes: it is not asked again during that time.

## Options

It is asked, for example, when creating or changing data sources, changing roles, resetting
another person's two-factor, creating tokens, setting up Telegram, allowing direct reports,
applying the workspace, **Apply project** and **Delete project**.

## Common problems

- If the password is wrong, the action is not done; try again.
