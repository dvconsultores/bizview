# Filters and periods

## What it is for

It adjusts what a report shows: the period, what it is compared with and which values it
includes.

## Who can use it

Anyone with access to the dashboard.

## How to

1. In the report open **Filters and controls** (on a phone, the **Filters** button).
2. In **Period** choose, for example, **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Month to date** or **Custom**
   with its **From** and **To** dates.
3. In **Compare with** choose **No comparison**, **Previous period** or **Previous year**.
4. In **Filters** choose one or more values; the list shows up to 200 matches and you can type
   to narrow it down.
5. Press **Apply filters**. Changes are not applied until then.

## Options

- **Active filters** shows what is applied; **Clear all** goes back to the start.
- Filters stay in the page address: sharing or reloading it keeps them.

## Common problems

- "The end date can't be earlier than the start date.": fix **From** and **To**.
