# Audit

## What it is for

It records sign-ins and administrative changes: who did what and when.

## Who can use it

Only the **Superuser**.

## How to

1. Open [Open Audit](app:/admin/auditoria).
2. Filter by **Event type**, for example sign-ins, user changes, sources or workspace applies.
3. Press **Load more** to see older events.

## Options

- Events are kept for 12 months.
- Sensitive actions (password confirmations, security changes, project applies) are always
  recorded.
