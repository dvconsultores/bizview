# Get help

## What it is for

The **Help** page gathers these guides, and the chat's `/ayuda` command answers about the app and
the Spec Kit method.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account sees **Help** with the topics of their role. The chat and the `/ayuda`
command are for the **Superuser**.

## How to

1. Open [Open Help](app:/ayuda) from **Help** in the menu.
2. Type in **Search help** or browse the sections.
3. Open a topic; at its end are the **Related topics**. **Back** returns to the list.

## Options

- **Superuser**: **Ask the assistant** opens the chat with `/ayuda` and the topic.
- In the chat, `/ayuda` alone shows the commands and what to do next; `/ayuda` followed by a
  topic (for example `/ayuda direct report`) explains it.
- You can also ask in your own words, for example "how do I share a dashboard?".
