# Administration

## What it is for

It is the entry to the administration tools: users and, for the **Superuser**, data,
integrations, projects, AI, security, tokens and audit.

## Who can use it

An **Administrator** sees **Users**. The **Superuser** also sees the other cards.

## How to

1. Open [Open Administration](app:/admin) from **Administration** in the menu.
2. Pick a card: **Users**, **Data sources**, **Workspace**, **Integrations**, **Access tokens**,
   **Security**, **Audit**, **Projects** or **Artificial intelligence**.

The counters of failing sources and integrations are on the home page.
