# Appearance and language

## What it is for

Choose the colour theme and the language BizView uses for screens, PDFs and emails.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account.

## How to

1. Open [Open My profile](app:/perfil).
2. In **Appearance** choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. **System** follows your device
   settings. The header button switches quickly between light and dark.
3. In **Language** choose **Español**, **English** or **Browser language**.

You can also change them from the **Account menu**. Your choice is saved in your account and
follows you to other devices.

## Common problems

- The language changes the app's texts, not the content people wrote: report titles and spec
  texts are shown as written (a report may carry optional English labels).
- If you see "Theme applied on this device, but could not be saved to your account.", choose it
  again when you are online.
