# Parameters and filters of the report

## What it is for

Changing what a report shows without covering the numbers: period, comparison, refresh frequency,
palette, theme and filter values. Everything applies as you change it and stays in the page address.

## Who can use it

Anyone with access to the dashboard.

## How to

1. On the report, the actions row has **Filters** and **Parameters**. Both open the same panel on the
   right (at the bottom on a phone) and select their tab.
2. In the **Parameters** tab each control has its own row, with its name above: **Period** (with
   **From** and **To** when you choose **Custom**), **Compare with**, **Refresh frequency**,
   **Palette** and **Report theme**.
3. In the **Filters** tab pick one or more values; the list shows up to 200 matches and you can type
   to narrow it.
4. Changes apply by themselves, after a quarter-second pause, while the dashboard stays visible
   behind the panel.
5. Press **Done** to close it, or **Clear** to return to the report's values.

## Options

- Under the title, chips show what is not at its default — period, comparison, palette and theme —
  plus one chip per applied filter. A chip's ✕ puts that value back where it started.
- **Palette** changes the colours for this view only and shows a row with the chosen theme's
  colours; **Use the installation's** is the default option. The report's theme is declared in the
  project, not from here.
- **Report theme** chooses **Automatic**, **Light** or **Dark** for your view; it does not change the
  report for others.
- **Refresh frequency** is stored per report on this device.
- What you applied stays in the address: reloading or sharing the link keeps it.
- Downloads live in **More actions** (⋯): **Download PDF** and **Download CSV** export the applied
  selection.

## Common problems

- "The end date cannot be before the start date.": fix **From** and **To**.
- "Wait for the report to finish loading.": the downloads appear once the report finished loading.
- A chip for a filter the report no longer has: the **Filters** panel says so and ignores it.
