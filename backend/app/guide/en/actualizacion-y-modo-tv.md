# Refresh and TV mode

## What it is for

It keeps a dashboard up to date without reloading the page and shows it full screen on a TV.

## Who can use it

Anyone with access to the dashboard.

## How to

1. In the report open **Auto refresh**.
2. Choose the **Frequency**: **Disabled**, **When new data arrives**, **Every 1 min**,
   **Every 5 min**, **Every 15 min** or **Every 60 min**.
3. For a TV or a monitor, press **TV mode** and then **Fullscreen**.
4. To go back, press **Exit TV mode** or the Esc key.

## Options

- Refreshing uses the applied filters; unapplied changes are kept.
- Direct reports refresh at most as often as their cache allows.
- If the installation was updated while the app was open, a notice appears with **Reload** (or
  **Not now** to stay on the version you have); the check is silent and only asks for the installed
  build.

## Common problems

- **No connection — retrying**: the connection was lost; the dashboard keeps trying and updates
  by itself when it is back.
