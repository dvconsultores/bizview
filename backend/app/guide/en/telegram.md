# Telegram

## What it is for

Link your Telegram account to sign in with one tap from your phone.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account, once the **Superuser** turned Telegram sign-in on. Setting up the bot is
for the **Superuser** only.

## How to

1. In [Open My profile](app:/perfil), **Telegram** card, press **Link Telegram**.
2. Open the link on your phone and confirm in Telegram.
3. The sign-in page shows **Sign in with Telegram**.

To remove it, press **Unlink** in the same card.

## Options

- **Superuser**: in [Open Security](app:/admin/seguridad), **Sign in with Telegram** card, type
  the **Bot username** and the **Bot credential**, press **Test** and then
  **Enable Telegram sign-in**.
- **Administrator**: in a user's page, **Remove Telegram link**.

## Common problems

- Telegram is not a second factor: if your account uses two-step verification, sign in with
  email, password and code.
- "Telegram is unavailable; sign in with your email.": the bot does not answer; use your email.
