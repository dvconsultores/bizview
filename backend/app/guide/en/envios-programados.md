# Scheduled sends

## What it is for

It emails a dashboard automatically every day, week or month.

## Who can use it

An **Administrator** or the **Superuser**, when the installation has email set up.

## How to

1. Open the dashboard and press **Scheduled sends**.
2. Press **New scheduled send**.
3. Choose the **Frequency**: **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**, the **Time** and, as needed,
   the **Day of the week** or the **Day of the month** (from 1 to 28).
4. Choose the **Period to send**, the recipients and whether to **Attach PDF**.
5. Press **Save**.

## Options

- In the list: **Pause**, **Resume**, **Edit** or **Delete** each send.
- Times are in the installation's time zone.
- A send that says **Managed by project** … comes from a distribution spec: it changes in the
  project's files; here you can only pause or resume it.
