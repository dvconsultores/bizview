# Home and favorites

## What it is for

The home page is your starting point: your favorite dashboards, the ones you opened recently and
a way to all the others.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account. Administrators also see counters of their installation, such as
**Data sources** and **Integrations** with failures.

## How to

1. Open [Open Home](app:/).
2. **Favorites** holds the dashboards you starred; **Recent**, the last ones you opened.
3. **See all dashboards** leads to the full list.

To add a favorite, tap the star of a dashboard in the **Dashboards** list.

## Common problems

- "You have no dashboards assigned yet. Contact your administrator.": an **Administrator** must
  give you access to a dashboard.
