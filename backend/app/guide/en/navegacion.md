# Find your way around

## What it is for

It explains where each section is and what the access-denied and missing-address screens do.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account. Each person sees only the sections of their role.

## How to

- On a computer, the side menu leads to **Home**, **Dashboards**, **Administration**,
  **Projects**, **Profile** and **Help**. **Administration** appears for administrators and
  **Projects** only for the **Superuser**.
- With **Collapse the menu** (below the side menu) it becomes a strip of icons and you gain the
  whole width for the dashboard or the project; **Expand the menu** brings the names back. The
  browser remembers the choice.
- On a phone, the bottom bar shows the main sections and the rest is in the **Account menu**
  (top right), together with **Sign out**.
- BizView opens no pop-up windows: confirmations and forms are views inside the page. The
  browser back button works as cancel.
- At the bottom of the page you find the **Source code** link and the installed version (BizView
  is AGPL free software).

## Common problems

- **Unauthorized**: the page exists but your role cannot open it. Ask an **Administrator** for
  access.
- **Page not found**: the address does not exist or was moved. Go back to
  [Open Home](app:/).
