# Sign in to BizView

## What it is for

Signing in gives you access to the dashboards assigned to you and, depending on your role, to
administration and projects.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account. Accounts are created by an **Administrator** or the **Superuser**;
there is no self sign-up.

## How to

1. Open [Open the sign-in page](app:/ingresar).
2. Type your **Email address** and your **Password**.
3. Press **Sign in**.
4. If your account has **Two-step verification**, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator
   app and press **Verify**. If you do not have your phone at hand, choose
   **Use a recovery code**.
5. If your password is temporary, BizView asks you to create a new one before you continue.

## Options

- **Sign in with Telegram**: shown when the installation has Telegram sign-in turned on and your
  account is linked.
- The language selector of the sign-in page changes the language on this device only.

## Common problems

- "Incorrect email or password.": check the email and the password. After 5 failed attempts the
  account is locked for 15 minutes, and attempts from the same address are limited too.
- "Forgot your password? Ask your administrator to reset it.": BizView sends no recovery emails;
  an **Administrator** resets the password.
- "Your session ended. Sign in again.": a session ends after 7 days without use or after 30 days.
