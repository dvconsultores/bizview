# Roles and permissions

## What it is for

It explains what each role can do and who to ask for access to a dashboard or a feature.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account.

## How to

BizView has three roles:

- **User**: sees the dashboards assigned to them, filters, exports and sends by email.
- **Administrator**: also manages **User** accounts, assigns dashboards, shares dashboards with
  links and schedules email sends.
- **Superuser**: there is only one per installation and it cannot be deactivated. It also manages
  administrators, data sources, integrations, the workspace, projects, AI, security, tokens and
  the audit.

To see a dashboard missing from your list, ask an **Administrator** for access: they assign it in
your user page.

## Common problems

- If a screen says **Unauthorized**, your role does not include it.
- An **Administrator** cannot change other administrators; the **Superuser** does that.
