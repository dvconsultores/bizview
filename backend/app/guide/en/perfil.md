# Your profile

## What it is for

It shows your details and gathers your account security, appearance and language.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an account.

## How to

1. Open [Open My profile](app:/perfil) from **Profile** in the menu.
2. In **My profile** you see your **Name**, your **Email** and your **Role**. An
   **Administrator** changes these details.
3. **Security** holds **Change password**.
4. The **Two-step verification** card lets you turn it on or off.
5. The **Telegram** card lets you **Link Telegram** or **Unlink**.
6. **Appearance** and **Language** change the app's theme and language.

To leave, open the **Account menu** and choose **Sign out**.
